Audio System (aud) Audaspace (pronounced “outer space”) is a high level audio library. aud.Sound('music.ogg') # Play the audio, this return a handle to control play/pause. handle = device.play(sound) # If the audio is not too big and will be used often you can buffer it. sound_buffered = aud.Sound.cache(sound) handle_buffered = device.play(sound_buffered) # Stop the sounds (otherwise they play until their ends). handle.stop() handle_buffered.stop() aud.AP_LOCATION Constant value 3
